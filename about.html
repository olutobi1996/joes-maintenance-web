Why Choose Prop-Up Services?

Prop-Up Services provides property maintenance and refurbishment services across Cambridge and the surrounding areas.

We work with homeowners, landlords and letting agents, undertaking everything from individual maintenance jobs and property repairs through to larger refurbishments and property improvements.

Our multi-skilled maintenance team allows us to cover a broad range of works through one point of contact, while specialist and appropriately qualified trades are used where required.

Our focus is simple: reliable service, good communication and work completed to a professional standard.
