General Maintenance FAQs

Can you deal with several jobs during one visit?

Yes. Our maintenance team covers a broad range of property works, so where practical we can complete multiple maintenance items during the same visit.

Do you undertake roofing and fencing work?

Yes. We can undertake fencing works and a range of roofing repairs. Larger or specialist roofing works can also be assessed and arranged where required.

Do you work in occupied rental properties?

Yes. We regularly carry out maintenance in occupied rental properties and can arrange access with landlords, agents or tenants as required.

Do you undertake small jobs?

Yes. We undertake individual repairs and maintenance jobs as well as larger programmes of work.

How quickly can you attend maintenance issues?

Response times depend on urgency, but we aim to attend as quickly as our schedule allows.

Do you offer regular maintenance plans?

Yes, we offer scheduled maintenance plans tailored to homeowners, landlords, and commercial properties.
