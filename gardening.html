Why It Matters for Landlords & Agents

An overgrown or neglected garden can put prospective tenants off before they even step inside. It affects kerb appeal, tenant satisfaction, and the overall impression of how well a property is managed. We work around your schedule and can coordinate directly with tenants where needed — keeping things simple for you.

Keeping outdoor spaces under control throughout a tenancy makes properties easier to let, easier to manage, and more attractive to view.

  • ✔ Improve kerb appeal ahead of viewings
  • ✔ Keep gardens tidy throughout tenancies
  • ✔ Prepare outdoor spaces for new tenants
  • ✔ Avoid end-of-tenancy disputes

Gardening FAQs

Do you offer one-off garden services?

Yes, we offer both one-off garden tidy-ups and regular maintenance packages depending on your needs.

Can you help with garden design?

Yes, we can assist with garden planning, planting, and layout improvements to suit your outdoor space and style.

Do you provide seasonal garden services?

Yes, we offer seasonal work including leaf clearance, planting, pruning, and general garden preparation throughout the year.

Do you remove garden waste?

Absolutely, we can remove and dispose of all green waste as part of our service, leaving your garden clean and tidy.

Can you maintain rental property gardens?

Yes, we regularly work with landlords and letting agents to keep outdoor spaces presentable and well maintained.

How often should garden maintenance be done?

Most gardens benefit from regular visits every 2–4 weeks, but we tailor schedules based on your property and requirements.
