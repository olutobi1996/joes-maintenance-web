Refurbishment FAQs

What size refurbishments do you undertake?

We undertake anything from individual room upgrades and redecorations to larger, full-property refurbishments.

Can you refurbish kitchens and bathrooms?

Yes. Kitchen and bathroom refurbishments form part of our wider refurbishment service, including associated decorating, flooring, tiling, plumbing and electrical works where required.

Can you manage several trades as part of the same project?

Yes. We can coordinate our maintenance team alongside specialist trades where required, allowing the different stages of a refurbishment to be managed together.

Do you provide free quotations?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotations following an assessment of the work required.

How long does a typical refurbishment take?

Timelines vary — small rooms take a few days, while full refurbishments can take several weeks.

Can you manage full property refurbishments?

Yes, we handle complete refurbishments from start to finish, coordinating all trades for a smooth result.
